About Stillspace

A student research prototype.

Stillspace offers optional check-ins, a conversation with different support styles, and a short reflection. Replies are generated by an AI model, or by built-in mock replies when the app runs in mock mode. The support style used for each reply is shown in the side panel.

Your answers and messages are saved on this computer for the research project, linked to your participant code rather than your name. To generate replies, chat text is sent to the AI provider (in mock mode it never leaves this computer).

The PHQ-9 total is shown only as a screening result, not a diagnosis. Item 9 can trigger a follow-up notice on its own. No clinical risk level is calculated. Additional questions never change the PHQ-9 score.

This is not therapy, diagnosis or an emergency service. In an emergency call 112 or Tele-MANAS 14416 (free, 24x7), or reach out to someone you trust.
