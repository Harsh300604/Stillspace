About Stillspace

A prototype for your research.

This downloadable interface demonstrates consent, surveys, mood check-ins and different support styles. All replies are prewritten examples; no AI service is connected.

Survey wording is provisional. No data is sent anywhere or retained after a refresh. This demo is not therapy or an emergency service. If you need urgent help, contact local emergency services or someone you trust.
